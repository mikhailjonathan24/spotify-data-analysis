Project documentation

Data, method & limitations

Data scope

The dashboard analyzes cleaned track records released from 1922 through 2020. The exact record count is shown in the first KPI.

The Top Tracks table and genre/key filters use a sample of up to 100 records ranked by popularity within this date range. Sample averages are labeled separately from full-dataset aggregates.

Method

Records without a track name or a parseable release year are excluded. Analysis is limited to 1922–2020. Audio features and popularity are averaged by release decade; artist counts are based on track records.

Popularity is a dataset snapshot, not historical popularity at the time of release. Correlation describes association and does not establish causation.
